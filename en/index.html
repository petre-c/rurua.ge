Notary services

The main notarial acts carried out at the office.

  • Contracts

    Sale, gift, exchange, lease, loan, mortgage and other transactions.

  • Real estate transactions

    Certification of property transactions and registration of ownership in the public registry.

  • Powers of attorney

    Drafting, certification and revocation of general and special powers of attorney.

  • Wills and inheritance

    Drafting and certification of wills, certificates of inheritance, acceptance and renunciation.

  • Signature certification

    Certifying signatures on applications, consents and other documents.

  • Certified copies

    Certifying that a copy or an extract matches the original document.

  • Translations

    Certifying a translation and the signature of the translator.

  • Parental consent

    Certifying a parent's consent for a minor to travel abroad.

  • Family law

    Prenuptial agreements, child support agreements, division of marital property.

  • Corporate documents

    Charters, minutes of shareholder meetings, share transfer agreements and other corporate papers.

  • Enforceable notarial act

    Enforcement of a monetary claim without going through court proceedings.

  • Deposits and attestation of facts

    Notarial deposit, attestation of legally relevant facts and of the submission of documents.

If what you need is not on this list, call and ask. You will be told whether it can be done here and which documents to bring.

What to bring

  • An ID card or passport for every party to the transaction.
  • Original documents, including copies and translations.
  • For a company, an extract from the registry of entrepreneurs and non-commercial legal entities.
  • For a real estate transaction, an extract from the public registry.
  • A valid power of attorney if a representative signs on someone's behalf.
