Reference: the rates in the decree

Fees are set by decree No. 507 of the Government of Georgia, 29 December 2011, as amended on 30 May 2022 by decree No. 272. The Georgian text is the authoritative one; this page is a translation for convenience.

Article 3: where the law requires notarial form, the fee can only be set by this decree. Where notarial form is not required, and for the notary's other services, the fee may be agreed. In setting it, the complexity of the act, the legal advice given, the technical work done and the time spent are taken into account.

Good to know

  • Every total is broken down: the notary fee, any extra cost and VAT are listed separately.
  • The transaction scale is progressive: the larger the value, the smaller the percentage on the upper band.
  • A certificate of inheritance costs half the transaction scale, calculated on each heir's share.
  • These figures are indicative. The exact amount depends on the document and is confirmed at the office.
